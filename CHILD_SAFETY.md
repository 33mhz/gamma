# Child Safety Standards

## Prohibited Content and Behavior
The following content and activities are strictly prohibited within the Gamma application and on the Pnut network when accessed through Gamma:
- The creation, distribution, or promotion of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
- Any behavior that targets, exploits, or endangers minors, including grooming, sextortion, or predatory activities.
- Content that sexualizes minors.

## Reporting
Report any content or behavior that violates these standards. Gamma provides an in-app reporting mechanism for posts:
1. Tap the "More" menu on any post.
2. Select "Report".
3. Choose the appropriate reason.

## Contact
